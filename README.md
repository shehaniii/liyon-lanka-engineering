# Liyon Lanka Engineering

A modern, responsive, and professional corporate website developed for **Liyon Lanka Engineering**, an engineering and construction company.

The website provides company information, services, completed projects, machinery, sustainability initiatives, career opportunities, and contact facilities through a clean and user-friendly interface.

## 🌐 Project Overview

**Liyon Lanka Engineering** is a full-featured corporate website designed to establish a strong digital presence for an engineering company.

The project focuses on:

* Professional corporate UI/UX
* Responsive design
* Clear presentation of engineering services
* Project portfolio showcase
* Machinery and equipment presentation
* Sustainability information
* Career application management
* Contact and inquiry management
* Administrative management pages
* Secure authentication-related pages
